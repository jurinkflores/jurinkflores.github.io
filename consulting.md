I offer rigorous quantitative and qualitative work, helping organisations generate insight and build lasting in-house capability. My experience includes working with charities, start-ups, regulators, and public bodies, across health, education, energy, environment, and human rights.

## What I do

**Evaluation design.** Designing practical, credible evaluations that fit real-world constraints, from descriptive approaches to quasi-experimental designs where randomised trials are not feasible. Developing outcome measures, data collection plans, and evidence frameworks that make clear what conclusions can and cannot be drawn.

**Quantitative and qualitative analysis.** Impact assessment, scenario and sensitivity modelling, econometric and causal inference analysis, distributional analysis, and regulatory and benchmark analysis.

**Strategy and planning.** Supporting organisations to develop evidence-led cases for investment, refine theories of change, align objectives with stakeholder priorities, and plan effective delivery.


## How I work

I design systems to be understood, maintained, and reused, not just delivered. This means collaborative design with client input from the outset, transparent methods rather than black-box outputs, and knowledge transfer embedded throughout the project.
